# Code of Conduct

## Our commitment

We want ClipMind to be a welcoming, useful project for contributors and users of
every background and experience level. Participation must be respectful,
constructive, and free from harassment.

## Expected behavior

- discuss ideas and code, not a person's worth or identity;
- assume good intent while still giving specific, evidence-based feedback;
- make room for newcomers and explain project constraints without gatekeeping;
- respect privacy, copyright, source-platform rules, and responsible disclosure;
- accept corrections and take responsibility for mistakes;
- keep disagreement focused on the product and its documented contracts.

## Unacceptable behavior

- harassment, threats, stalking, sexualized attention, or discriminatory remarks;
- insults, personal attacks, trolling, or sustained disruption;
- publishing another person's private information or credentials;
- sharing downloaded creator media, faces, transcripts, or other sensitive
  evidence without permission and a clear project need;
- pressuring contributors to weaken privacy, security, or evidence-integrity
  boundaries;
- retaliation against someone who reports a concern in good faith.

## Scope

This code applies in repository issues, pull requests, reviews, discussions,
project chat, events, and other spaces where someone represents ClipMind.

## Enforcement

Report conduct concerns privately to the repository maintainers through an
available private GitHub channel. Do not include sensitive personal information
in a public issue. Maintainers may edit or remove content, warn participants,
temporarily restrict participation, or permanently ban conduct that threatens a
safe and productive community.

Enforcement decisions should be proportionate, documented privately, and avoid
unnecessary disclosure about reporters or affected people. A maintainer involved
in a complaint should recuse themselves when another maintainer is available.

## Attribution

This policy is inspired by the Contributor Covenant and adapted to ClipMind's
privacy-sensitive media and evidence domain.
